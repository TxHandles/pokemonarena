GYM LEADER PICTURES
===================
A picture of a gym leader here shows up in their gym's intro (they walk out onto the arena) and
in battle (a cut-in when the battle starts and when they're down to their last Pokemon).

The file name is the leader's name, matched against the "trainer =" line in story.txt:
    Brock.png    Misty.png    Surge.png (Lt. Surge)    Erika.png    Koga.png    Sabrina.png
    Blaine.png   Giovanni.png
    Falkner.png  Bugsy.png    Whitney.png    Morty.png    Chuck.png    Jasmine.png    Pryce.png    Clair.png

Use a PNG with a transparent background, facing the camera or to the left (they stand on the
right of the screen). Any size works; around 80 pixels tall matches the others.
