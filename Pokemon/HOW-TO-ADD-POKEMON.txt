ADDING POKEMON
==============

Every folder in here is one Pokemon: a pokemon.txt plus its sprite images.
The game reads them each time it starts - no rebuilding needed.

  Pokemon\
    _TEMPLATE\        <- copy this to start a new Pokemon (ignored by the game)
    Bulbasaur\        pokemon.txt + sprites
    Charmander\       pokemon.txt + sprites
    Squirtle\         pokemon.txt (built-in placeholder art until sprites are added)
    Pikachu\          "

ADD A NEW POKEMON (you can do this yourself - no new download needed)
  1. Copy "_TEMPLATE" and rename the copy to the Pokemon's name (e.g. Ivysaur).
  2. Put its sprites in the folder.
  3. Fill in pokemon.txt (every setting is explained inside it).
  4. Start the game and check Pokemon\load_log.txt for anything it didn't understand.
  New base forms show up in Quick Battle right away and as Story Mode rewards.

ADDING SPRITES TO SQUIRTLE / PIKACHU
  Drop the images into the folder and delete the "placeholder_art = ..." line.

EVOLUTION CHAINS
  On the earlier form:   evolves_to = <folder name of the next form>
                         evolve_level = 16
  Stone evolution:        evolve_item = Vaporeon, Water Stone
  Friendship evolution:   evolve_friendship = Crobat, 160
  Trade evolution:        evolve_trade = Alakazam   (evolves when traded to your co-op partner)
  The next form is just another folder. Chains can be any length.
  In Story Mode the evolution happens the moment the level is reached - even in
  the middle of a battle - or right after the battle.

ABILITIES AND POKEDEX
  The original 151 get their real abilities and Pokedex entries automatically.
  For your own Pokemon (or to change them):
    abilities      = Overgrow            (one or two, comma separated)
    hidden_ability = Chlorophyll         (1 in 20 caught have it)
    category  = Seed                     height = 0.7  (m)    weight = 6.9  (kg)
    dex_entry = A strange seed was planted on its back at birth.
  Ability names must match the list in Party & Box / the Pokedex (the games' names).

LEVEL-UP MOVES
  learn = 7, Leech Seed           (by name from MOVE-LIST.txt)
  learn = 20, Razor Wind, Normal, Spread, 40, 1.5, crit     (your own move)
  learn = evo, Fire Blast         (learned the moment it evolves into this Pokemon)
  The original 151 use their real level-up moves from Scarlet/Violet (or Brilliant
  Diamond/Shining Pearl for Pokemon that aren't in Scarlet/Violet).
  Every move from the main games is in MOVE-LIST.txt.
  A Pokemon can know 4 moves. When it would learn a 5th, the game asks which
  move to forget after the battle.

TM MOVES
  tm = Thunderbolt, Thunder, Thunder Wave, Protect     (any number of tm lines)
  The moves it can learn from TMs, which are bought in the Poke Mart's TMs tab
  (stronger TMs unlock with badges) and taught from Party & Box. TMs use the
  Scarlet/Violet TM list (TM001-TM229). The original 151 list exactly what they can
  learn in Scarlet/Violet (or their most recent game if they aren't in it).
  The Move Relearner (also in the Poke Mart) teaches back level-up moves up to the
  Pokemon's level and any move it forgot.

SPRITE IMAGES
  - PNG (also JPG, BMP, GIF). WEBP files need converting to PNG first
    (open in Paint and Save As PNG). Transparent backgrounds work best; a solid
    background color is removed automatically.
  - Grid-chart pictures (colored squares with numbered rows/columns and a color
    legend) are read square by square, with or without numbers in the squares.
  - Name each file with its direction: front/mae/down, back/ushiro/up,
    left/hidari, right/migi, plus _1 / _2 for the two walking frames.
    Missing directions are filled in (left and right mirror each other).
  - A picture with no direction in the name becomes the menu portrait.
  - Small sprites (about 16-32 px) show at the same size as the built-ins.
    Use "size = 1.3" etc. to make one bigger or smaller.

STATS AND LEVELS
  Copy the base stats straight from the Pokedex: hp, attack, defense, sp_attack,
  sp_defense, speed. The game scales them up for battle.
  Physical moves (Melee, Dash, Burst, Wave, Blink, Burrow) use attack vs defense;
  special moves (Shot, Spread, Beam, Lob, Zone, Strike, Rain) use sp_attack vs
  sp_defense. Add "physical" or "special" to a custom move to choose yourself.
  Stats also grow with level: level 5 = 55%, level 50 = 100%, level 100 = 150%.
  Free play (Quick Battle / 2 Players) uses level 50 for everyone.

STARTERS
  "starter = yes" makes a Pokemon one of the Story Mode starter choices.
  Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle are tagged; tag more to offer more.

THE BUILT-IN TEST CREATURES
  Furbit, Cindercub, Ripplit, Joltelly and the others are the original test
  creatures (one per type). They stay in the game until real Pokemon replace
  them. To take one over with a Pokemon, add "replaces = Joltelly" (etc.) to
  that Pokemon's pokemon.txt.

AFTER STARTING THE GAME
  - load_log.txt     what was loaded from every folder, and any mistakes found
  - MOVE-LIST.txt    every move you can use by name, with what it does
  - <folder>\converted\   the sprites the game read from your charts
